# Research receipt

## Result

What was actually observed, derived, or computed? State the scope, artifact references, and uncertainties. If no experiment ran, state that explicitly.

## Strongest attempted falsification

What concrete failure condition was tested? Was the test valid and admissible? Distinguish an attempted falsification from a proposed one.

## Orthogonal check

What different failure pathway did the check address? Record the outcome, shared dependencies, unknown dependencies, and what this check cannot establish.

## Consequence

Which hypothesis or assumption changed? Which explanations remain indistinguishable? How does this affect the original research target rather than only an internal proxy?

## Next question

Specify the cheapest feasible experiment most likely to discriminate the important remaining alternatives. State its predictions and why it outranks the nearest alternative test.
